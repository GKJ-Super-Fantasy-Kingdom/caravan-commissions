# 0.1.0

First release for Super Fantasy Kingdom.

- Compare today's caravan cards with a three-item pool from another kingdom. Review the material cost and available stock before paying.
- Choose one of the promised items at the next eligible caravan, defer delivery, or exchange an unavailable order without a second payment.
- Orders and spent visits follow the run checkpoint through tavern saves, backup restores, and same-day dawn replays.
- The desk uses native card artwork and button styling at both UI scales, with mouse, keyboard, and gamepad navigation.
- Ordinary draws retain item weights and support short or empty pools. Commission payment and delivery verify the material withdrawal and the new item drop when other mods modify those operations.
